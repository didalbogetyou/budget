How this works
  • Tax rates: 2025–26 baseline = 16% on $18,201–$45,000. 2026–27 = 15%. 2027–28+ = 14%.
  • LITO: $700 max, tapers 5c/$ then 1.5c/$, gone at $66,667. Applied to both before and after.
  • $1,000 instant deduction: From 2026–27. Worth (1,000 − your existing claims) × marginal rate.
  • $250 WATO: Non-refundable offset from 2027–28 for income from work.
  • Negative gearing: Quarantined losses valued at your marginal rate.
  • CGT: Old = 50% discount. New = indexed cost base with 30% min tax on the real gain. Annualised over years held.
  • Trust: Compares per-beneficiary tax under flow-through (income split evenly across beneficiaries) vs trustee 30% minimum with non-refundable credits. The reform bites when distributions go to multiple low-rate beneficiaries.
  • EV FBT: Annual benefit lost ≈ 19.5% of vehicle value × 75% (25% discount remains).
  • Not financial advice. Excludes HECS/HELP, MLS, franking, SAPTO, state taxes.
